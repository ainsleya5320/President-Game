# The American Electorate

Press **V** in Unreal, or choose **Voters** from the presidential screen. Choose a Democratic or Republican administration once per term. Existing saves retain their quarter, policies, relationships and history.

The atlas contains 3,143 counties and county equivalents, 51 state/DC outlines and 231 named cities. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a county, double-click to focus its state, or search by state, city, full county name (optionally `, ST`) or five-digit county FIPS. Alaska and Hawaii are insets; the uninhabited Northwestern Hawaiian chain has a separately compressed strip above the main islands. Insets do not share the continental scale.

Eight layers show Woodard cultural regions, projected two-party vote, turnout proxy, household income, college attainment, poverty, presidential approval and historical 2024 two-party vote. The Road to 270 view presents electoral weight independently of land area. City markers always denote named cities, never countries or states.

## Sources and vintages

- **Cultural classification:** Colin Woodard's [2017 Portland Press Herald analysis](https://www.centralmaine.com/2017/01/06/the-american-nations-in-the-2016-presidential-election/) and its [published county table](https://specialprojects.pressherald.com/americannations/county_results.csv). This implements that historical table, not the newer [request-only 2025 Nationhood Lab classification](https://www.nationhoodlab.org/the-american-nations-regions-across-north-america/). Read [American Nations](https://colinwoodard.com/books/american-nations/) and [Nations Apart](https://colinwoodard.com/books/nations-apart/) for the author's framework. Descriptions and game coefficients here are original interpretations, not quotations or survey estimates, and are not endorsed by the author.
- **Geography:** U.S. Census [2021 county boundaries, 1:500,000](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2021/shp/cb_2021_us_county_500k.zip) and [state boundaries](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2021/shp/cb_2021_us_state_500k.zip). These retain Connecticut's eight historical counties for consistent joins. Continental geometry uses Albers equal-area projection (EPSG:5070), with Alaska EPSG:3338.
- **City points:** [Natural Earth populated places, 1:10m, v5.1.2](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/), public domain. The U.S. subset includes scale ranks 0–6.
- **Population and economic inputs:** Census-derived tables served by the [USDA ERS Atlas of Rural and Small-Town America](https://ers.usda.gov/data-products/atlas-of-rural-and-small-town-america/documentation). Population and unemployment are 2021; age and population background shares are 2020; education, household income, poverty and tenure use 2017–2021 ACS. Income is in the source's 2021 dollars. Rural/urban classification uses the 2013 continuum codes. Raw service metadata and file hashes are recorded by the fetch script.
- **County election baseline:** [MIT Election Data and Science Lab, County Presidential Election Returns 2000–2024](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ), CC0. The build uses a [public mirror of the July 2025 release](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/9t8/cmsc320_project/master/countypres_2000-2024.csv), not Dataverse's later February 2026 release. This older input contains reporting/code problems; reconciliation is described below and recorded in `build.json`.
- **Certified statewide totals:** [MEDSL 2024 official state returns](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MEDSL/2024-elections-official/main/2024-president-state.csv). Democratic and Republican county vote volumes are separately calibrated to these state totals. The calibrated historical allocation reproduces **226 Democratic / 312 Republican electors**.
- **Electoral College:** [National Archives allocation for 2024 and 2028](https://www.archives.gov/electoral-college/allocation), plus [Maine](https://www.archives.gov/files/electoral-college/2024/ascertainment-maine.pdf) and [Nebraska](https://www.archives.gov/files/electoral-college/2024/ascertainment-nebraska.pdf) certificates for district baselines. There are 538 electors and 270 are needed to win.

## Reconciliation

County identifiers join to Census geometry. When a source FIPS does not match the reported state/name, the builder repairs it using the state and normalized county name; it preserves valid identifiers for same-name counties and independent cities. Rhode Island municipality returns aggregate into counties. Reported TOTAL rows take precedence over vote-mode subtotals to avoid double counting. The complete join-correction list, state calibration factors, input URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in `build.json`.

Alaska election districts do not correspond to its boroughs/census areas. Those 30 county equivalents and Hawaii's Kalawao use state-share estimates; their county panels explicitly disclose the fallback. Do not treat their historical colors as measured local results.

The Woodard table equally divides Cook County, Illinois between Yankeedom and Midlands, and Orleans Parish, Louisiana between New France and Deep South. The builder preserves those mixtures. It reconciles renamed Kusilvak and Oglala Lakota counties and the split of Valdez-Cordova into Chugach and Copper River. Missing Kalawao follows Hawaii's Greater Polynesia classification. Missing Franklin County, Alabama is assigned Greater Appalachia as an explicit authoring assumption. The fourteen displayed zones include the original eleven nations, Spanish Caribbean, Greater Polynesia, and Washington DC's Federal Entity; DC is a reference zone, not a claim that it is a cultural nation.

## Simulation, not a scientific election forecast

Each county contains eight synthetic age/education/housing cohorts: age 18–64 or 65+, college degree or no BA, homeowner or renter. Their shares are products of measured marginal distributions. They are **mutually exclusive joint cohorts across overlapping demographic dimensions**, not measured survey microdata. Independence is an approximation: homeownership is a household statistic, degree attainment covers ages 25+, and both are used as proxies for adult composition. Sampling error, citizenship, registration and eligibility are not modeled. Background race/ethnicity shares are descriptive only; no race coefficient assigns political preference.

Approval weights service quality, economic conditions, local regional outcomes, existing interest-group mood and cultural policy fit. Senior cohorts place more weight on health; renters on housing; education and local industries affect issue salience. Cultural response coefficients, these weights and all effect sizes are authored game mechanics. Regional cultures influence preferences without making everyone within a region identical.

Party support begins with the county's calibrated 2024 two-party baseline, adds a symmetric incumbency effect, and responds to approval and organizing. Approval and party support are separate quantities. Turnout responds to demographic proxies, trust, satisfaction/dissatisfaction and field organizing, changing each county's weight. Reported turnout is a **two-party ballots/adult-population proxy**, not official eligible-voter turnout. Cohort baseline party loyalty is county-level, because no validated joint county survey is included.

One field operation per open quarter costs two political capital, gives a local enthusiasm/turnout benefit and a smaller statewide benefit, and decays each quarter. It is available for either party under identical rules. Existing visits, crises and regional public-service outcomes continue to feed into counties.

Presidential outcomes use state winner-take-all allocation, except Maine and Nebraska: two statewide electors and one per congressional district. District projections use their certified baseline plus simulated statewide swing, rather than precinct-level district demographics. Exact state ties are unallocated; 269–269 or any result without 270 is shown as no majority. A congressional contingent election is not simulated. Midterms remain a simplified national approval-based congressional mandate. Third parties, Senate/House district elections and primary contests are future work.

## Rebuilding and validation

The game runs offline with the checked-in outputs. `Scripts/fetch_us_electorate.py` caches public inputs under ignored `Saved/ElectorateSources`; `Scripts/build_us_electorate.py` produces the simulation JSON, triangulated map JSON, review PNG and provenance manifest. Python dependencies: numpy, pyshp, shapely, pyproj, mapbox-earcut, Pillow; the local tool bundle is under `Saved/AtlasTools`.

Run `Scripts/test_us_atlas.py` for geometry/joins and `Prototype/test_county_electorate.cjs` for electoral calibration, party choice, policy response, turnout, organizing and migration. The full JavaScript/C++ parity trace also exercises these actions across complete presidencies. `Scripts/test_electoral_atlas.py` performs save-preserving native integration checks during Play in Editor.
