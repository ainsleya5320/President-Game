A playable design sketch

Four years. One office. No perfect choices.

Lead a fictional administration through twelve monthly briefings. Every decision spends time or political capital, changes public trust and fiscal room, and may return months later.

Walk through the Oval Office, approach the desk, and open the briefing. Each run has small uncertain setbacks. Your decisions leave a record.

Original game concept inspired by political simulators and travel-survival games. Fictional scenario; no real candidates or forecasts.
