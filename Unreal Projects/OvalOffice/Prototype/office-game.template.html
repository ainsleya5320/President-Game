A four-year political simulation

Four years. No perfect choices.

Lead a fictional administration through sixteen quarters and a full four-year term. Set policy across the economy, health, education, housing, justice and energy. Results build over time, reshape the budget, and affect different voters in different ways.

Walk through the Oval Office or board Air Force One. Read your briefing at a desk or around the aircraft’s conference table. Face quarterly dilemmas, a midterm vote, and a final election. Your decisions leave a record.

Original game concept inspired by political simulators and travel-survival games. Fictional scenario; no real candidates or forecasts.
